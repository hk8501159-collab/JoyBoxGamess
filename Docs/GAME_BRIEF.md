# Game brief

## Working concept

JoyBoxGamess is a third-person open-world exploration game that moves between an Indian city and a nearby village. The first playable version should make the setting readable and enjoyable to explore before the team expands its systems or map size.

## Agreed direction

- **Setting:** A fictional Indian city connected by road to a village.
- **Platforms:** PC and Android.
- **Camera and play:** Third-person movement and exploration.
- **Planned systems:** Buildings, vehicles, and missions.

## Prototype goal

Build one compact route from a city street to a village lane. The player should be able to walk and sprint along it, recognize the two areas, and complete one short mission. Keep the first environment small enough to run well on a mid-range Android device.

## Out of scope for the first prototype

- A seamless state-sized open world.
- Online multiplayer or a full economy.
- Final art, licensed locations, and production-ready vehicle physics.

## Decisions to make during development

- Final title, story, and protagonist.
- Specific region, language mix, and visual references.
- Vehicle types and whether driving is arcade-style or simulation-style.
- Android minimum device target and quality settings.
- Mission progression and save format.
